Take Care of Your Pet

Buddy the dog and Mittens the cat live in a house in the middle of town. Love them up, explore the city, and visit the Pet Shop for dogs, cats, bunnies, chickens, birds, snakes and spiders.

Walk with the arrow keys, the arrow buttons, or by clicking the ground or map. Tap a pet to pet, feed, play and name it. Caring for pets earns coins and fills their level circle. Press Hide & Seek and go find everyone! Every pet can go to Obedience School, and the Pet Resort pampers pets while you play. At night, pesky raccoons sneak around. Shoo them, or tell a dog to scare them off! Don't let raccoons take over 3 trash cans, or the wolf will sneak in and hide your pets! Visit Mayor Maple at City Hall for missions, and the Hat Store for something fancy. Forest trails ring the whole town, one for every kind of pet. Each one needs a well-trained guide! Hop on your bike (press B) to zoom around town. Need help? Mr. Appletree at City Hall can explain anything.
