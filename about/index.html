About the arcade

What this is

Elk Leaf Arcade is a small, growing collection of games you can play right in your web browser, on a phone, tablet or laptop. There is nothing to download or install: pick a game and start playing.

There is something for everyone: look after pets in a 3D city, explore an elven forest and face a dragon, stomp raccoons as a chicken, race a squirrel across floating islands, build a house block by block, or pass the phone around for an archery contest with friends.

Our promise

  • FreeEvery game, all of it.
  • No adsNothing pops up while you play.
  • No accountsNo sign-up, no passwords.
  • No trackingWe don't collect anything about you.

Fair play and healthy habits

Games are better when everyone gets a turn and everyone knows when to stop. The turn timer gives each player a nickname and a time limit. When a turn ends, a chime rings, the game saves, and the arcade asks the player to pass the device on. Grown-ups can lock it with a PIN.

Where your progress goes

Games that remember your progress save it in your own browser, on your own device. It never gets sent to us. That also means progress doesn't follow you to another phone or computer, and clearing your browser's site data will reset it.

New games

New games and new levels show up from time to time. Look for the New badge on the menu.
